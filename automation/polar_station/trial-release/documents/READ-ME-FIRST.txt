Polar Station — Apple Silicon Mac trial

Drag Polar Station.app to Applications, then open it. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. If macOS requires approval, use its normal Open Anyway controls; do not disable system security.

The original app passed a limited 15-second macOS 26 launch check. This does not establish all GUI, permission, older-system or upgrade scenarios.
